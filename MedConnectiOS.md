1. Introduction

MedConnect Group 2025, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Med Connect mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use the App.

For the iOS version of the App, the service is limited to patient and nursing-professional interactions, appointment management, messaging, payments, notifications, and related account features.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide and operate the App, including account creation, sign-in, appointment booking, messaging, and related support features.
- Connect patients with nursing professionals and manage schedules, booking confirmations, and service coordination.
- Enable in-app messaging between users and nursing professionals.
- Process payments securely through supported payment providers.
- Show nearby nursing professionals and support location-based services when location permission is granted.
- Send notifications for booking updates, reminders, support messages, and account activity.
- Improve app performance, reliability, and user experience.
- Respond to customer support requests and maintain app security.
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.
